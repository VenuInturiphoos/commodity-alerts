How MCX Daily Price Limits (DPL) & Circuit Bands Work

MCX contracts are bounded by daily limits designed to curb artificial manipulation and panic flash-crashes. These limits are calculated daily strictly from the contract's Previous Daily Settlement Price (DSP).

1. Initial Slab (Base Filter)

Trading begins each morning bound by an initial price band (typically 3% for Gold, 4% for Silver, Crude & Copper, and 6% for Natural Gas). Orders beyond this band are rejected by the RMS.

2. 15-Min Cooling-Off Period

If the market experiences sudden extreme volatility and touches the initial slab, the exchange activates a 15-minute cooling-off period or automatic relaxation step to absorb news.

3. Enhanced Slab & International Parity

Post cooling-off, the limit automatically expands to the enhanced slab (up to 6% - 9%). For internationally linked energy/metals, additional 3% steps can be relaxed to match COMEX/NYMEX global parity.
